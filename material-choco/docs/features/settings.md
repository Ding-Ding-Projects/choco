# Settings that explain themselves

Each setting says what it does and whether its value is the built-in default, and can be reset on its own.

## What it is

The **Settings** page has these tabs: Language, Appearance, Accessibility tools, Sound and narration, Focus modes, Timed settings, Sign in and locks, Data and export, About and changelog. Every setting has a sentence of explanation and a line that reads either **Built-in default** with the value, or **Changed on this device** with a **Reset** link.

Settings are stored in the browser, per device. They are not sent to the hub.

## How to use it

- Change a setting; it applies at once.
- **Reset** on a changed setting returns it to the default.
- **Data and export** has **Export settings**, **Import settings** and **Reset every setting**, which asks for the [two key confirmation](two-key-confirmation.md). It also shows the global options added to every command you run.
- **About and changelog** shows the version, build time and the changelog.
- Any setting can be found by name in the [command palette](command-palette.md), which jumps to it.

## What was checked

End to end checks `bilingual mode shows the Cantonese line` and `dark theme applies` change settings through this page.

## Known limits

- Settings do not follow you to another browser or device, except through export and import.
- Clearing the site's storage resets them.
