Tabs

Every page is a tab in a strip on the left. All are open at first; close, pin and reopen them as you like.

On this page
  1. What it is
  2. How to use it
  3. What was checked
  4. Known limits

What it is

The strip lists the open pages with an icon and a name. Each tab has a close button, and pinned tabs sit at the top with a pin mark. New tab at the bottom reopens closed pages. The order, the pins and the closed tabs are remembered in the browser.

How to use it

  • Click a tab to open its page. The up and down arrow keys move between tabs.
  • Drag a tab onto another tab to move it there, or focus a tab and press Alt+ArrowUp or Alt+ArrowDown. Pinned tabs stay at the top and are reordered among themselves.
  • Alt+PageDown and Alt+PageUp go to the next and previous open tab.
  • Right click a tab, or press Shift+F10 on it, for pin, close, move and reopen actions.
  • New tab lists the closed pages and also offers Open every tab.
  • Going to a closed page another way, for example through the palette, reopens its tab.

On narrow screens the strip is hidden behind the menu button, and a bottom bar holds the most used pages.

What was checked

End to end checks a tab can be moved with the keyboard and the order persists and a tab can be moved by drag and drop. Closing, pinning and reopening have no dedicated named check.

Known limits

  • No grouping and no docking to another edge.
  • Tab choices are stored per browser.
