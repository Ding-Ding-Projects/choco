Sound and narration

Short action sounds, and an optional voice that reads job results aloud.

On this page
  1. What it is
  2. How to use it
  3. What was checked
  4. Known limits

What it is

Action sounds are brief tones generated in the browser when a job is queued, succeeds or fails, and with the full-screen results. They are on by default, have a volume setting, and never carry information that is not also on screen.

The spoken narrator is off by default. When on, it reads the result of your jobs using the speech voices installed on your device. You choose the narration language (English, Cantonese or both), a voice for each, rate and pitch.

How to use it

Settings, Sound and narration. Turning action sounds on plays a sample.

What was checked

No dedicated named check. Sound cannot be asserted by the headless run.

Known limits

  • The narrator reads job results only, not pages or notifications in general.
  • Voices depend on the device. Without a Hong Kong Cantonese voice, the Cantonese line is skipped.
  • The Low stimulation focus mode silences action sounds.

Source of this page: docs/features/sound-and-narration.md. It describes version 0.1 of the app. See the status page for what has and has not been proven.
